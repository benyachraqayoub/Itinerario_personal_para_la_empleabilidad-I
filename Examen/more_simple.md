# Cheat Sheet: Exact Spanish Sentences for the PRL Exam

Practice copying these short, high-yield sentences by hand. They use the exact vocabulary from the slides and the audio that your teacher expects to see on the exam paper.

---

## 🏢 1. Organismos y la OIT (Organizations)

*   **Sentence to write:** La OIT es un organismo de la ONU que mejora las condiciones laborales en el mundo.
    *   *Meaning:* The ILO is a UN organization that improves working conditions worldwide.
*   **Sentence to write:** Los organismos nacionales son el INSST y la Inspección de Trabajo (ITSS).
    *   *Meaning:* The national organizations are the INSST and the Labor Inspection (ITSS).

---

## 📋 2. El Plan de Prevención (The Prevention Plan)

*   **Sentence to write:** El plan de prevención recoge la política preventiva de la empresa. Es un documento vivo.
    *   *Meaning:* The prevention plan includes the company's preventive policy. It is a living document.
*   **Sentence to write:** Incluye los datos de la empresa, el proceso productivo y la estructura organizativa.
    *   *Meaning:* It includes company data, the production process, and the organizational structure.

---

## ⚡ 3. El Riesgo Laboral (Labor Risk)

*   **Sentence to write:** El riesgo laboral es la probabilidad de sufrir un daño derivado del trabajo.
    *   *Meaning:* Labor risk is the probability of suffering damage derived from work.
*   **Sentence to write:** Se valora según la probabilidad, la gravedad del daño y la exposición.
    *   *Meaning:* It is valued according to probability, severity of the damage, and exposure.

---

## ⚖️ 4. Responsabilidades y la LISOS (Sanctions & Laws)

*   **Sentence to write:** Existen tres responsabilidades: administrativa, civil y penal.
    *   *Meaning:* There are three responsibilities: administrative, civil, and criminal.
*   **Sentence to write:** La LISOS clasifica las infracciones en tres grados: leves, graves y muy graves.
    *   *Meaning:* The LISOS classifies infractions into three degrees: light, serious, and very serious.

---

## 🛡️ 5. Medidas de Protección: EPC vs EPI (Protections)

*   **Sentence to write:** Las medidas colectivas tienen prioridad sobre las individuales.
    *   *Meaning:* Collective measures have priority over individual ones.
*   **Sentence to write:** El EPC protege a varios trabajadores a la vez, como una barandilla o una red.
    *   *Meaning:* The EPC protects several workers at the same time, like a handrail or a net.
*   **Sentence to write:** El EPI es de uso individual para un trabajador, como un casco o unos guantes.
    *   *Meaning:* The EPI is for individual use for one worker, like a helmet or gloves.

---

## 🦺 6. Tipos de Agentes (Types of Agents)

*   **Sentence to write:** Los agentes físicos son el ruido, las vibraciones y la temperatura.
    *   *Meaning:* Physical agents are noise, vibrations, and temperature.
*   **Sentence to write:** Los agentes químicos son los gases, vapores y sustancias tóxicas.
    *   *Meaning:* Chemical agents are gases, vapors, and toxic substances.
*   **Sentence to write:** Los agentes biológicos son los virus, las bacterias y los hongos.
    *   *Meaning:* Biological agents are viruses, bacteria, and fungi.

---

## ✍️ 7. Vocabulary Keywords (Perfect for short answers)

*   **Empresario** = Employer / Business owner
*   **Trabajador / Trabajadora** = Worker / Employee
*   **Deber de protección** = Duty to protect (The employer's main job)
*   **Vigilancia de la salud** = Health surveillance (Medical checkups)
*   **Voluntaria** = Voluntary (Medical checkups are usually voluntary for the worker)
*   **Organigrama** = Organization chart / Hierarchy diagram
*   **Daño** = Harm / Damage
